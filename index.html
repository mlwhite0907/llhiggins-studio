Good to know.

Can I use my current domain?

Yes, if you control the domain and your host supports the finished site. We check your setup before agreeing to the handoff.

What do I need to provide?

Your business name, services, contact details and any photos or logo you have permission to use. We can work with rough notes.

Is an online store included?

The $249 scope is an informational website. It can link to your existing store, booking page or menu. A new store or booking system is outside this scope.

Do you take on other small projects?

We also make a one- or two-page menu or service sheet for $59 and offer a scoped website launch check for $49. See the service details.

Do you have client references?

Not yet. The example on this page is a demonstration we control. We do not invent clients, reviews or years of experience. If a project requires named references or a tool we have not used on a paid job, we say so before you hire us.
