Agreeing on the work

Before asking for payment, we confirm the project scope, price, expected delivery and included revision or retest. Prices are in U.S. dollars. Any additional work or third-party costs, such as hosting or a domain, are agreed separately.

We begin after payment is confirmed and we have the information and access needed for the agreed work. If anything is missing or timing changes, we will let you know.

What each service includes

The one-page website includes up to five sections and 600 words from your information, one revision and portable files for compatible hosting you own. The service sheet or menu includes one or two pages with up to 20 items, a PDF, an editable source file and one revision using your approved wording and prices.

The website launch check covers up to five agreed pages in one browser at desktop and mobile-sized viewport widths. It includes a report of confirmed issues, one agreed test-environment form check and one retest after changes. Code repairs, security or accessibility certification, and real-device testing are not included. A check cannot guarantee that every issue has been found.

Your information and authorization

Please provide accurate project information and content you have permission to use. You keep the rights you already have in materials you provide, and you allow us to use them to complete your project. For website checks, you must own the site or have authority to request the agreed testing. Provide an approved test environment and a way to verify the form receipt if a form check is included.

Please do not send passwords, card details or sensitive personal information through our contact form or email.

Delivery and review

We deliver the files or report described in the agreed scope. Contact us if something is missing or incorrect, or to request the included revision or retest. For a website check, you may rebrand and share the report with your client while keeping its test date, coverage and limitations intact.

Cancellation, refunds and privacy

Our refund and cancellation policy explains how to cancel or raise a concern. Our privacy notice explains how we handle information shared with us. You can contact us with questions about your project.
