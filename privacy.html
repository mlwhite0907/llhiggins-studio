YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy, put simply.

Here is how information shared with LLHiggins is used when you make an inquiry or work with us.

QUESTIONS?Contact us ↗

What we receive

When you contact us, you may provide your name, email address, business information and project details. We use this information to reply, discuss and deliver agreed work, provide support and maintain business records.

Services that help us

We use Google Forms and Google email for inquiries and support. We may use other service providers to help operate, create and deliver your project; they receive information needed to provide those services. Our website host may also receive basic technical information, such as an IP address, to deliver and protect the site.

If a card payment is offered, enter payment details on the payment provider’s secure checkout, not in our inquiry form or an email. Please do not send passwords, card details or sensitive personal information through the contact form.

Keeping and correcting information

We keep information for as long as needed for your inquiry, project, support, business records and legal obligations. You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion. We may need to keep some records for legal, accounting or security reasons.
